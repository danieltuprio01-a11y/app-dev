# Favorite Movies

Here are some of my favorite movies:

## 1. Fight Club
**Genre:** Drama  
**Director:** David Fincher  
**Release Year:** 1999  
**Synopsis:** Fight Club follows an unnamed narrator who forms an underground fight club as a form of self-expression and rebellion against the consumerist society.

## 2. Avengers Movies
**Genre:** Action, Superhero  
**Director:** Various (Marvel Cinematic Universe)  
**Release Year:** 2012 - 2019  
**Synopsis:** The Avengers movies follow a group of superheroes who team up to save the world from different threats, leading to massive battles and emotional moments.

## 3. Now You See Me
**Genre:** Crime, Thriller  
**Director:** Louis Leterrier  
**Release Year:** 2013  
**Synopsis:** A group of illusionists pulls off bank heists during their performances, challenging the authorities to catch them using their magic skills.
